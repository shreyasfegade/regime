    Hidden market regimes for the NIFTY 50

    Search above for any NIFTY 50 stock, or start with an index.

    Only NIFTY 50 stocks are covered. Every stock gets a full multi-start model fit, walk-forward refits and robustness checks; keeping to the 50 largest, most liquid names keeps that complete and refreshed every week.

    REGIME classifies each day into one of four hidden regimes with a Gaussian hidden Markov model fitted to that stock's own history. Prices: Yahoo Finance daily data. Universe: the NIFTY 50 constituents (NSE), ranked by AMFI's market-cap list. Data as of —, refreshed weekly.
